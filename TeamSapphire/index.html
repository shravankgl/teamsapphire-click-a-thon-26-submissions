Released in the final hours and run cold through the same command. Bundle → — diagnosis, the numbers behind it, and the trace that proves our system generated it.

How it works

Raw MergeTree → three dictionaries resolving nine dimensions at insert time → two SummingMergeTree rollups. The obvious fully-crossed rollup was built first and measured at 7,247,816 rows from 7.2M events — it compressed nothing. The unpivoted grain that replaced it is 53,760 rows, 167× smaller. One investigation runs 111 queries over 934,006,552 rows in ~37s of ClickHouse time.
