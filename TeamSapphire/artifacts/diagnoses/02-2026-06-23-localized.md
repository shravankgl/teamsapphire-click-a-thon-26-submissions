### In plain language

Between 2026-06-23 00:00:00 and 2026-06-25 23:00:00 — a 72-hour window — revenue moved -0.2%. The movement sits almost entirely in fill rate, which fell from a baseline of 0.7854 to 0.7508, a -4.4% change accounting for 22.2 of the share of movement. Requests actually rose 4.4% (808,472 to 843,741), eCPM was flat at 2.4756 versus 2.4748 (+0.03%), and render rate was unchanged at 0.9801.

This was localized, not broad. Android 15 moved -44.9% against the global -4.4%, carrying 100% of the unexplained movement and 88% of the whole incident; EU moved -7.1% and carries 15% of the incident. The shape is a switch, not a slide: fill rate for Android 15 went from 0.7846 to 0.4439 within the single hour at 2026-06-23 00:00:00, with 96% of the total movement landing in that one hour, and it reversed just as sharply at 2026-06-26 00:00:00 (0.4310 to 0.7962, 100% step). Both transitions land exactly on a day boundary and the episode lasted exactly 3 days. Requests, render rate and eCPM held steady throughout. Interpretation: this is consistent with something that schedules in days — a campaign flight, a config push, a nightly job — with a defined end date, rather than an organic failure that was later fixed.

Ruled out or cleared: publisher_tier (all 3 values within 0.2% of the global -4.4%, largest outlier explains 1.6%), ad_format (all 5 within 0.4%, largest outlier 1.5%), and category (all 7 within 0.5%, largest outlier 1.4%) all moved uniformly. Device model is inconclusive — Redmi Note 12 leads but at 31% of unexplained movement, under the 50% concentration bar; country is likewise inconclusive — ES leads at 29%, and explains only 7% of the incident, under the 10% materiality bar. Vertical and campaign_type are not applicable, since every value holds the same fill rate. The evidence rules out a traffic or tracking problem: request volume was unchanged, so the same demand arrived and was handled differently. Next step is to pull anything with a 2026-06-23 to 2026-06-26 date range scoped to Android 15 — bidder or demand-partner targeting rules, SDK/config flights, and EU-region eligibility settings.
