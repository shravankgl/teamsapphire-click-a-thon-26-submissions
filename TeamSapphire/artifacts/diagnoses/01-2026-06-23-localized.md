A secondary concentration appears in region = EU, which moved -7.1% and accounts for 15% of the incident. The shape is a clean step, not a slide: fill rate for Android 15 went from 0.7846 to 0.4439 within the single hour at 2026-06-23 00:00:00, with 96% of the total movement landing in that hour, exactly on a day boundary. It reversed just as sharply at 2026-06-26 00:00:00 (0.4310 to 0.7962, step fraction 1.0) after exactly 3 days, while requests, render_rate and eCPM held steady throughout. Interpretation: a step on a day boundary that reverses after a whole number of days is consistent with something that schedules in days — a campaign flight with a defined end date, a config push, or a nightly job — rather than an organic degradation or a fix.

The evidence also rules out a traffic or tracking problem: request volume was unchanged, so the same demand arrived and was handled differently. Next, pull the Android 15 demand-side configuration and any campaign flights or targeting rules with a 2026-06-23 to 2026-06-26 date range, and check the EU concentration against the same schedule.
