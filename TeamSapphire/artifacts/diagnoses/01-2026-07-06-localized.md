### In plain language

Between 2026-07-06 00:00:00 and 2026-07-10 21:00:00 — a 118-hour window — revenue fell 2.12%. The movement is almost entirely a pricing effect: eCPM came in at 2.2403 against a baseline of 2.4656, a 9.14% decline, accounting for 4.4643 of the share of movement. Requests actually rose 8.59% (1,483,235 vs 1,365,958 baseline), offsetting part of the damage at -3.8379 share. Fill rate was essentially flat at 0.7685 vs 0.7746 (-0.79%), and render rate was unchanged at 0.9799 vs 0.9800 (-0.01%).

The incident is localized. Video ad format moved -18.0% against -9.4% globally, carrying 100% of the unexplained movement and 29% of the whole incident. Two correlated slices show up beneath it: Android 12 at -17.2% versus -9.3% globally (73% of unexplained movement, 20% of the incident) and iPhone 14 at -22.3% versus -9.2% globally (66% of unexplained movement, 18% of the incident). The shape of the transition in video eCPM is gradual, not a single step: the onset at 2026-07-06 00:00:00 took it from 6.520218 to 6.102399 (-0.417818), with the largest single hour carrying only 34.31% of the change. It recovers gradually from 2026-07-10 23:00:00, 4.126668 to 4.297747 (+0.171079, step fraction 15.48%). Interpretation: the gradual profile is consistent with a rollout or progressive degradation rather than one instantaneous change, but the onset lands exactly on a day boundary, which is consistent with something that schedules in days — a campaign flight, a config push, a nightly job — rather than an organic failure.

Checked and cleared: publisher_tier was uniform, all 3 values within 0.5% of the global -9.1%, largest outlier explaining 1.1% of the incident; category uniform across all 7 values within 3.7% of the global -9.3%, largest outlier 0.8%; campaign_type uniform across all 4 values within 9.1% of -9.1%, largest outlier 0.6%; vertical uniform across all 8 values within 9.1% of -9.1%, largest outlier 0.3%. Region and country were inconclusive — NAM and US lead their dimensions but each carries 0% of the unexplained movement, below the 50% concentration bar, so the movement is spread rather than concentrated geographically. Fill rate and render rate held steady throughout, so this is not a delivery or rendering failure. Next step: look at video demand on the 07-06 day boundary — campaign flights starting or ending, bidder or floor-price config pushed that day, and whether the Android 12 / iPhone 14 concentration reflects a specific video SDK version or inventory pool rather than independent device effects.
